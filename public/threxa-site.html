The problem

Operations don't scale
by adding people.

Every Shopify order in India touches five systems — checkout, accounting, logistics, customer messaging, and reviews. Each handoff is manual. As order volume grows, so does the cost of running it.

// 01
Manual order processing

Orders are logged into spreadsheets, copied to Tally, shipped via Shiprocket, and confirmed on WhatsApp — by hand. The work is necessary, repetitive, and impossible to scale beyond a point.

// 02
Revenue lost to silence

Carts are abandoned, COD orders go unconfirmed, and post-purchase communication ends at the order confirmation. Every gap is a customer who leaves quietly.

// 03
No system of record

Critical operational data sits across Shopify, WhatsApp threads, Tally, and a founder's memory. Decisions get made on instinct rather than what the numbers actually show.
